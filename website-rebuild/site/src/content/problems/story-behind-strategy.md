The business has changed. Acquisitions, divestments, a new direction. The way it is described, inside and outside, has not caught up, and the gap is starting to cost.

## Why it goes wrong

The default move is a rebrand, because the gap feels like an identity problem. It is usually a narrative problem. A new logo over an old story changes nothing a stakeholder can hear. The work is to rebuild the account of what the organisation now is, why it changed and where it is going, in language the leadership will actually use.

## What the engagement looks like

We work from the strategy documents and from interviews with the people who have to tell the story, then draft, test and sharpen the narrative against the audiences that matter most. Weeks, not months.

The client is left holding one account of the business that the chair, the chief executive and the front line can all tell without contradiction.
