The call has come. The story is running with or without the organisation, and the next four hours decide which version survives.

## Why it goes wrong

The default move is to say too much or nothing at all. Both hand the story to someone else. The organisations that come through keep their statement inside what they know to be true, decline to speculate and never let the response become a second story. That discipline is hard to find for the first time at 16:00 on a Friday.

## What the engagement looks like

Immediate senior counsel, in the room or on the phone, for as long as it takes. Thirty years of this, including running crisis management across EMEA, is why the counsel stays calm when the room does not.

The client is left with the version of events that was true, told first and told once.
