Something is building. A regulatory change, a campaign, a planning dispute, a slow-burning grievance. It has not broken yet, and the window to shape it is closing.

## Why it goes wrong

The default move is to wait, because nothing has happened and there are always louder priorities. But an issue on a long fuse is the one form of reputational risk where the timeline is a gift. Waiting converts a manageable issue into a crisis with a press deadline, and by then every option is worse and more expensive.

## What the engagement looks like

We map the issue, the actors and the likely trigger points, then agree the position the organisation can hold under pressure, because a position you cannot hold is a liability. Counsel runs alongside the issue for as long as the fuse burns, usually a monthly rhythm with surge capacity when it moves.

The client is left holding a position that has been stress-tested before it was needed, and the option of acting early, which is the option most organisations discover too late they no longer have.
