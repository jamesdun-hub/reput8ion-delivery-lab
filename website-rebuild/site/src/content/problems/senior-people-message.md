The strategy is sound and the materials are fine. Put a senior person in front of a journalist, an Oireachtas committee or a town hall and it does not land.

## Why it goes wrong

The default move is presentation training: a day of camera practice and a folder of techniques. It fails because delivery is rarely the real problem. The real problem is that the speaker does not fully believe the message, or has never been made to defend it against someone paid to take it apart. Polish on top of doubt reads as evasion, and audiences smell it.

## What the engagement looks like

We start with the message, not the performer. Once the speaker can argue the position and mean it, we pressure-test the delivery against realistic hostile questioning, measured and reviewed, until it holds. Usually two or three sessions per speaker, spaced so the work beds in.

The client is left with senior people who can carry the organisation's story under pressure, not perform it under studio conditions.
